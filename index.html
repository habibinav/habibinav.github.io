About Abhinav

Abhinav is a final-year Computer Science student at RSET in Kochi. He works mostly in applied machine learning, and his capstone is a platform that detects deepfakes and AI-generated video and explains why it thinks so.

Outside the lab he builds small, opinionated web apps, the latest being Kflix, a screening room for two that streams a film peer-to-peer so nothing is ever uploaded. He also solves competitive programming problems, sometimes in Python 2.7 with no imports because that's what the judge runs.

The Film Roll

Developing Kflix

Each frame is still undeveloped. Scratch it to bring the picture up.

Frame 01 · Lobby

Watch the same film, miles apart

Name yourself, open a room, send one link. The poster wall drifts in random order behind you.

Frame 02 · Input 1

Drop a film anywhere

The file plays from your disk through a local URL, so the first frame shows up in milliseconds whatever its size. It never touches the server.

Frame 03 · Pause

Either of you can stop the tape

Play, pause or seek from either side. The other follows, with a small note saying who did it. Drift stayed under 100 ms in testing.

Frame 04 · CC

Subtitles, shared

Load .srt, .vtt or .ass and both people get the track. Nudge the timing with G and H; the fix is shared too.
